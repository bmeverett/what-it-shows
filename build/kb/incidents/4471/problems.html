The three problems

parser: the shared parser read dates month first. Every tenant writes the day first, so “7 April” came out as “4 July”. This is the bug #4471 named. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: booking-v3 told people to type the month first. Its date field said MM/DD/YYYY. People at day-first tenants on booking-v3 typed what the hint said; the form read it the tenant’s way, and the date flipped. The parser fix did not touch this. Won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired.

one-day: dates move by a day, not a month. Tuesday shows as Monday; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday; an invite arrives before its booking. That is a time-zone conversion, not a swapped day and month, so the parser fix does not reach it. The desk had noted these reports (desk-notes.json) and filed them under parser.

#4471 is closed, but one-day is not fixed. Keep its ticket open, and send new one-day reports there, not to a closed ticket.

How the reports were placed

  • One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. They count once.
  • By tenant, not by intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), so this page groups by that.
  • Counted where the desk filed them. A report the desk read as a one-day move stays in the parser count, as on the #4471 page, and is also counted under one-day (problems.ts).

The numbers above are computed from the ticket export at render time; when the desk merges more reports, the table follows.
