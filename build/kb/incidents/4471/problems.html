The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant writes the day first, so “7 April” comes out as “4 July”. This is the bug #4471 names.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first. Its date field says MM/DD/YYYY. People at day-first tenants on booking-v3 type what the hint says; the form reads it the tenant’s way, and the date flips. The parser fix leaves this one broken: the hint itself is wrong.

one-day: dates move by a day, not a month. Tuesday shows as Monday; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday; an invite arrives before its booking. That is a time-zone conversion, not a swapped day and month. The desk had noted these reports (desk-notes.json), but the #4471 page still counts them under parser.

Shipping the parser fix and closing #4471 leaves the hint and one-day reports with no ticket. Keep each problem on its own ticket until its own fix ships.

How the reports were placed

  • One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. They count once.
  • By tenant, not by intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), so this page groups by that.
  • A one-day move is its own problem. A report the desk read as a one-day move leaves parser and goes under one-day (problems.ts).

The numbers above are computed from the ticket export at render time; when the desk merges more reports, the table follows.
